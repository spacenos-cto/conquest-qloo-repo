# Jury rehearsal: our pitch, played to the judges' own audiences

We ran Conquest's own tool on the jury: each judge's audience from Qloo, 1,000 simulated fans, one pitch line at a time.

**Read this honestly.** The fans here are the free local respondents: their scores come from how well the pitch's words overlap each audience's Qloo tastes. No AI language model was used, so these numbers show direction, not a forecast. Re-run on the hosted build with `CONQUEST_AI=claude` to get the fans' answers in their own words.

## What to say on stage

1. **Open for Jason with growth and investing:** "An AI growth strategist for the 50 million people who earn from an audience, with investment-grade signals from real taste data." That framing doubled the love share with his crowd (26% → 55%).
2. **Then turn to Cedric with comedy:** "For comedians: your next tour, planned like a game. The city that laughs hardest, the room to book, the comic to co-headline with." Comedy framing helps his crowd more than place names do.
3. **Show, don't list:** the panel reacts to concrete nouns (investing, stand-up), not adjectives. Keep every line concrete.
4. **Todd Boehly:** lean on sports and venues (teams, stadiums, fan territory across markets), since Qloo can't profile his audience for us.
