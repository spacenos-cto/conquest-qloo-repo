# Jury rehearsal: winning each judge

We played our pitch to each judge's own audience with Conquest's tool: their real fans from Qloo, 1,000 simulated fans, one line at a time.

**Correction (live re-run).** An earlier version of this page said Qloo had nothing on Abrams: that run had silently fallen back to sample data. Re-run on live Qloo, J.J. Abrams has a full audience.

**How to read the numbers.** These fans are the free local ones: their scores come from how well a line's words overlap each audience's Qloo tastes. No AI model was used, so they show direction, not a forecast. The scale also tops out early (about 3.6/5): the game only rewards a line for hitting up to two of a fan's tastes. A higher "love it" share is the signal to watch.

| Everyone at once | "A growth strategist for anyone with fans: comedians, podcasters, teams…" | only **26–33%** |

**The lesson:** one generic line loses every judge. A 10-second opener aimed at each judge's own world roughly doubles the love share. So the pitch should have one opener per judge, not one opener for the room.

## 3. How to win each judge (pitch + what to show)

1. **Jason (the $25K):**
   - Say: "Every creator is a startup. Conquest is their growth team."
   - Show: play as Jason. His All-In channel gets X-rayed, and the tool honestly reports that his views follow news hooks more than taste fit.
   - Close on the market: about 50 million people earn from an audience, and almost none can afford a strategist.
2. **Cedric:**
   - Say: "Your fans in Atlanta and Chicago are waiting."
   - Show: play as Cedric. The briefing names his home turf and his real rivals for fans. Then battle for a city and watch the crowd switch sides.
3. **Todd Boehly:**
   - Click **Play as the LA Dodgers** (new on the landing page). His rivals on the map are ex-Dodgers players (Justin Turner, Corey Seager, Enrique Hernández), and the X-ray reads the Dodgers' real YouTube channel.
   - Say: "Every team is fighting for the same fans, city by city."
4. **Qloo's CTO:** show where the data comes from.
   - About 100 Qloo calls per campaign, each one logged with receipts.
   - The panel refuses to simulate an audience Qloo can't see (Todd Boehly himself is the live example).
   - Everything simulated is labelled as simulated.
5. **Everyone (the judging criteria):**
   - Usefulness: real moves that go on your calendar.
   - Technical execution: AI agents that call Qloo, Trends, weather and Wikipedia as tools.
   - Clear sourcing: every number cites its source.
   - Responsible data: refusals and honest labels.
   - Reproducibility: the jury tour and cached runs.
