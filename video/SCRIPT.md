# Conquest: submission video script (2:41)

The story in one line: **creators guess where to grow → Conquest turns your real audience into a map and an army of agents → one END WEEK of real loot → the finished tour pack → why that beats the alternatives.**

Everything on screen is the real app (`npm run serve`, live Qloo cache), played as **Cedric the Entertainer** through the one-click judge path. The only things that aren't app footage are two text cards (the problem, the numbers) and the end card.

The captions below are burned into `conquest-demo.mp4`. The **Say** column is an optional voiceover that matches them, about 125 words a minute (see README.md for how to record it).
